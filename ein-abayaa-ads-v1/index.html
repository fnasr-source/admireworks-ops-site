Priority 1 : Fix this first

The tracking is broken, two ways at once

We dug in live. Two separate problems stack on top of each other and make every number unreliable. Both are fixable, and we have store access to lead it.

Problem 1: the store sends no shopping signals

Your store is on a custom theme. We added a product to cart live and it fired nothing: no Google, Meta, Snapchat or TikTok event, and the product page had no tracking data at all. Every app shows "active" but is starved of input.

Problem 2: purchases get counted twice

The same Google Analytics account is fired by both Zid's built-in app and your Tag Manager on the same page. That double-fire is the main reason orders read ~4x too high.

Honest caveat: the over-count is not a clean double (orders read ~4.3x high, revenue ~3.5x), so there are likely some duplicate plus low-value or cross-domain phantom orders too. We confirm exactly which during the fix.

Add-to-cart should be the tallest step before purchase. Here it is below all of them. That inversion is the signature of the break. It also collapsed Meta add-to-cart from 1,389 in April to 29 in June.

The fix (we have store access and will lead it)

  1. Make Tag Manager the single owner. Remove Zid's built-in Google Analytics so only one purchase tag fires per order. Kills most of the over-count.
  2. Stamp every purchase with the Zid order ID so Google and Meta automatically drop any duplicate. The safety net.
  3. Get the custom theme to send shopping events (view, add-to-cart, checkout, purchase) so the pixels finally have something to listen to. Revives the dead add-to-cart.
  4. Route every pixel through Tag Manager off those events, and turn off each platform's built-in auto-event so nothing double-fires again.
  5. Switch on Google Ads conversions and re-point Snapchat to the real purchase event. Both show zero today only because of this gap.
  6. Treat Zid as truth until a clean week passes. After the fix, Google Analytics should land within ~5 to 10% of Zid, and we also check for Tabby / cross-domain phantom orders.
Independently confirmed. A Google Ads audit by your own team found the same break from the Google side: the new Performance Max campaign (live since 18 June, scaling fast on Maximize Conversions) has recorded 0 purchase conversions ever, so it is optimizing toward a sale signal that never arrives. The same fix resolves it and unlocks Shopping/PMax. (Our own pull matches the audit: 202 clicks, SAR 419, 0 conversions.)

The copy lesson

Words that sell here

  • Sensory Arabic story copy is a click magnet. The fabric-and-mood ads had the highest click rate. Use it on every new color drop.
  • Sell the feeling, not the specs. Tie each color to a moment: sunset, morning calm, garden shade. That is your organic-IG voice.
  • Minimal copy still sells when the photo carries it, but it caps clicks.
  • Never run template / placeholder copy. The {{product.name}} catalog ad is the proof: zero orders.
  • Match depth to audience: rich story to cold, tight color-led to warm retargeting.
