Where Black Modesty stands today

You built a brand people love. The quality is real, the five-star reviews are real, and the orders come through word of mouth, which is the hardest kind of trust to earn. Now campaigns are about to start, and the next stage brings more orders, more conversations, and more moving pieces.

Three things will hold that stage back if they stay as they are:

  • The tracking behind the store and the ad accounts is disconnected. When campaigns launch, you will not be able to see clearly what the money brings back.
  • The business runs on a manual sheet and on your phone. Orders from the website and orders from WhatsApp live in different places, and nothing shows the full picture in one look.
  • Everything waits for you. Every reply, every follow-up, every post. That caps how far the brand can grow, and it puts the whole business on your shoulders alone.
This proposal fixes all three.

What we will build and run for you

A complete growth setup, delivered and operated by Admireworks as one partnership:

  • A correct measurement setup, before campaigns spend money. We reconnect and verify all the tracking behind the store and the advertising platforms (Google, Meta, and the product feed) and clean out the old, broken setups left from previous work. When campaigns start, every pound spent is measurable.
  • An expert eye on the advertising. Your media buyer runs the campaigns. We review them on a set schedule (the setup, the targeting, the budgets, and the pages the ads lead to) and give a quality check before every launch. A second pair of expert eyes, so the budget stays safe and effective.
  • One system for the whole business. Instead of the sheet, every order and every customer (from the website and from WhatsApp) lives in one organized place with clear screens: today's orders, where customers come from, what is selling, who is coming back. Anyone on your team can use it.
  • Faster replies, without losing your personal touch. A smart assistant answers the common questions (prices, sizes, availability, order status) instantly, day and night, on a dedicated store number, and hands the conversation to you for custom orders and personal moments. Your current WhatsApp number keeps working exactly as it does today. Every conversation (both numbers, Instagram, and Messenger) lands in one inbox, so no customer is ever missed.
  • A store that sells better. Practical improvements to the website: clearer sizing, easier ways to ask and order through WhatsApp, your five-star reviews displayed where they convince, complete-the-look suggestions, and a cleaner, more visual look.

All of Black Modesty's data (orders, customers, conversations, content) belongs to Black Modesty, and a complete export is available at any time at no cost. Copy, designs, and content produced specifically for Black Modesty are Black Modesty's IP.
  3. Fees. 12,000 EGP per month at the partnership rate. First term of 4 months invoiced upfront (48,000 EGP, Invoice AWI-202606-CGNXT). Months 5 onward invoiced monthly in advance.
  4. Renewal. Month-to-month after the first term. Either party may cancel with 30 days written notice; the effective end date is the next billing date after the notice period.
  5. Pass-through costs. Ad spend, WhatsApp message fees, the media buyer's fees, and store platform fees are paid by Black Modesty directly and are never marked up by Admireworks.
  6. Advisory boundary. Admireworks reviews and advises on advertising but does not operate the ad account and is not responsible for the media buyer's execution or for advertising results.
  7. Operational boundary. Products, suppliers, pricing, and order fulfillment remain fully with Black Modesty, along with final approval on anything customers see.
  8. Payment. First invoice due on signature, Net 7, delivered by email and WhatsApp with full payment details. Services may be paused if an invoice remains unpaid 7 days after its due date.
  9. Data protection. Black Modesty is the data controller; Admireworks is the data processor. Customer data is processed only to operate the system, in line with applicable data-protection law including the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law.
  10. Confidentiality. Mutual confidentiality on all non-public business information. Survives the partnership for 3 years.
  11. Liability. Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 3 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. The cap does not apply to confidentiality or data-protection breaches.
  12. Good faith and governing law. Anything that needs working out, we work out first through good-faith discussion. Formally, this agreement is governed by the laws of the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), UAE, and Egyptian data-protection law applies to Egypt-resident customer data.
