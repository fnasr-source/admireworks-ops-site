How these numbers were built
  • Every sale is a real, non-cancelled Shopify order. Meta's own reported purchases are never used as revenue, they run well above what Shopify can confirm.
  • Shopify's API only shows the last 60 days of orders. Orders before that window use Admireworks' dated 21 Jul audit instead of a live read.
  • The 32% margin is an estimate on VAT-inclusive sales, which slightly overstates true profit. Your stated range is 30-35%.
  • Bank currency fees are estimated at 3% of Meta card charges. The Shopify subscription, courier fees and product cost are not included, you pay those directly.
  • Current live ad budget is 1,200 EGP a day (1,000 sales plus 200 brand awareness).
