Proof it works

A whole brand, built on the card.

علامة كاملة بُنيت على البطاقة

Pyrrha, in Canada, casts talismans from antique wax seals and ships each one with a printed card explaining its meaning. That single device turned jewelry into gifting, and lets a silver piece sell for $275 to $350, far above its metal value.

The model is proven. It is simply not Egyptian yet. That space is ours.
