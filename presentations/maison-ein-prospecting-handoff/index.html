Every copy fix made this pass

Full adversarial GULF-RETAIL review plus a diff against the live (not just drafted) creative text. Two of these five were only visible on the live ad objects, not the source file.

  • الكشكشة to الطيات
    Client flagged this exact word live ("الكشكشة؟"). Market-sewing term, wrong register for quiet luxury. Concept 2, primary text.
  • تلمسها to تلمسيها
    Live creative had drifted from the source draft to a non-feminine form. Restored consistent direct feminine address. Concept 2, opening line.
  • صوت عالِف to صوت عالٍ
    A corrupted word in the live creative, invisible until diffed against the source file. Concept 3, closing line.
  • اكتشفي المجموعة كاملة to تعرّفي على المجموعة كاملة
    "Discover the collection" is a stock AI-tell phrase. Echoes the card's own link-description verb instead. Concept 3, closing CTA line.
  • فصل جديد من الفخامة to فصل جديد، حضور جديد
    Bare luxury-noun declaration with no concrete detail. Carries the brand's own "حضور" metaphor instead. Carousel, Card 2.
