1. Content Production and Editing

Edit raw video recordings provided by Mr. Fouad into finished content for Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms as required
Create vertical and horizontal video formats appropriate to each platform
Add captions, text overlays, and visual elements consistent with the MOND brand identity
Ensure all content meets the established quality and brand standards

2. Daily Accountability and Recording Coordination

Maintain daily communication with Mr. Fouad regarding recording activity
Track all video recordings produced and ensure timely delivery of content to the production pipeline
Hold Mr. Fouad accountable to the agreed recording schedule (target 5 days per week or as otherwise agreed)
Follow up proactively when recording sessions are missed or delayed

3. Content Distribution and Posting

Manage and post content to MOND's Instagram and TikTok accounts (@mond.hq) according to the agreed content calendar
Schedule and publish posts at optimal times for audience engagement
Maintain consistent posting frequency and platform-specific formatting

4. LinkedIn Content Drafting

Draft LinkedIn posts based on raw video content and approved themes
Submit drafts to Mr. Fouad for personal voice rewriting and final approval before publication
Follow up to ensure LinkedIn content is published on schedule

5. Content Calendar Planning

Collaborate with team to develop weekly and monthly content calendars
Maintain a content backlog and ensure consistent supply of post ideas
Adjust the calendar based on performance data and business priorities

6. Performance Tracking and Reporting

Track content performance metrics including reach, engagement, follower growth, and conversion to MOND Score completions
Provide weekly written reports summarizing what is working, what is not, and recommended adjustments
Identify content patterns and trends that inform future strategy

7. Creative Direction and Ideation

Contribute original content ideas, formats, hooks, and angles
Stay current on platform trends, emerging content formats, and competitor activity in the marketing infrastructure space
Propose new content series, recurring formats, or experimental concepts for review and approval

8. Brand Voice and Quality Standards

Ensure all published content aligns with the MOND brand voice, methodology (the Nano Diamond Method, the 4 Cs Framework), and visual identity
Maintain consistency in tone, terminology, and design across all channels
Review work for quality, accuracy, and adherence to standards before publication

9. Other Responsibilities

Perform additional content-related duties as reasonably required to support the growth of MOND
Participate in weekly one-on-one meetings with Mr. Fouad to align on priorities and discuss strategic direction
