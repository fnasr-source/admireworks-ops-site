Format rules: 9 numbered sections, Title Case headings, 2-4 sub-bullets each,
sentence case, NO terminal period, written as ownership statements.

1. Client Relationship Management

Serve as the primary day-to-day contact for clients across the Admireworks portfolio, building trust through consistent and proactive communication
Understand each client's business objectives, marketing goals, and brand voice in depth
Anticipate client needs and surface them to the internal team before they become escalations
Lead recurring client check-ins and ensure every conversation is captured and followed up on

2. Campaign Coordination

Translate client objectives into clear briefs for the internal strategy, creative, and media teams
Coordinate end-to-end campaign execution from kickoff through launch and reporting
Maintain a single source of truth for timelines, deliverables, dependencies, and approvals per client
Identify execution risks early and resolve them before they impact the client experience

3. Client Communication and Reporting

Own all written and verbal client communication, ensuring clarity, professionalism, and on-brand tone
Produce and deliver weekly and monthly performance reports tailored to each client's KPIs
Walk clients through results in scheduled review meetings and convert insights into next actions

4. Project Execution and Quality Control

Ensure every deliverable meets Admireworks' quality standard before it reaches the client
Run final quality checks on creative, copy, landing pages, ad sets, and trafficking before launch
Hold internal teams accountable to agreed scope, timelines, and brand guidelines

5. Internal Team Coordination

Coordinate daily with strategy, creative, media, and tech teams to keep each account moving
Run internal account stand-ups and weekly account-health reviews with the relevant team leads
Escalate cross-functional blockers to the Managing Partner with proposed solutions, not problems

6. Performance Tracking and Account Health

Monitor campaign performance, account health signals, and client satisfaction on an ongoing basis
Flag at-risk accounts to the Managing Partner the moment retention signals slip, with a written recovery plan
Maintain a current account-health view for the Admireworks portfolio and review it with the Managing Partner

7. Process Improvement and Account Growth

Identify upsell, cross-sell, and scope-expansion opportunities within each client account
Document and refine the Account Management playbook, SOPs, and templates as you go
Contribute to onboarding new clients, including kickoff structure and 30-60-90 day plans

8. Brand Standards and Compliance

Ensure all client-facing work aligns with the Admireworks brand voice, design system, and quality bar
Apply the StoryBrand framework and the Admireworks direct-response playbook consistently across the portfolio
Review every outbound deliverable for accuracy, tone, and adherence to each client's brand guidelines before sign-off

9. Other Responsibilities

Perform additional account-management duties as reasonably required to support the growth of Admireworks
Participate in weekly one-on-one meetings with the Managing Partner to align on priorities and discuss strategic direction
