# Bonus / variable-compensation block

VERBATIM from the real Sally Tarek letter (2026-05-16), extracted 2026-08-08.
REPLACES the 2026-08-07 reconstruction, which had lost the real figures and
described the crisis-recovery bonus as vaguely "awarded case-by-case" and the
churn bonus as "under the agreed target" with no numbers.

## Where it goes

Inline under `Compensation`, directly after the salary sentence, which then
ends in a COLON rather than a period:

> The starting salary for this position will be 20,000 EGP per month, plus a performance bonus tied directly to client outcomes:

## The only real example in the corpus (account management)

Reproduce the SHAPE; the OPERATOR supplies the numbers. These figures were
negotiated for one hire and are not a default.

```
1. Client retention milestones, paid as each client under your management reaches each milestone during your first year:
 – 500 EGP per account at 3 months retained
 – 1,500 EGP per account at 6 months retained
 – 3,000 EGP per account at 12 months retained

2. Crisis recovery bonus: 3,000 EGP for every at-risk client formally retained for at least 3 additional months after the threat.

3. Annual excellence bonus:
 – 20,000 EGP if year-end portfolio churn is below 10%
 – 30,000 EGP if year-end portfolio churn is below 5%

Bonuses are paid as outcomes are achieved, not on a regular cadence. After your first year, we will review your role, scope, and compensation together based on your performance and the natural growth of the team.
```

## Formatting details that matter

- Top-level items are plain-text `1.` `2.` `3.`, NOT a Word list.
- Sub-items use an EN DASH (`–`, U+2013) preceded by ONE leading space. Not a
  bullet glyph, not a hyphen.
- The closing paragraph ("Bonuses are paid as outcomes are achieved...") is
  part of the block.

## The post-year-one review sentence is OPT-IN

> After your first year, we will review your role, scope, and compensation together based on your performance and the natural growth of the team.

A soft commitment with real consequences. Include only when actually intended.
