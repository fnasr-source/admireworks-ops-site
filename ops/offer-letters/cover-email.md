rule applies to marketing/client mail, not to an HR offer, which is a named
person writing to a named person.

## Body (verbatim)

```
Dear {FirstName},

We hope all is well.

We're happy to extend an offer for you to join our team at Admireworks as
{Role Title}.

Please find attached your offer letter, outlining the details of this
opportunity.
To accept this offer, please sign and return a copy of the attached offer
letter.

We look forward to welcoming you aboard.

Feel free to reach out if you have any questions.

All the Best,
```
