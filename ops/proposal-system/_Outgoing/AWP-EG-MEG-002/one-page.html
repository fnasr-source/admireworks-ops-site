2. Influencer programme

Included in the same fee, not an extra.

  • Build a shortlist of Egyptian car and automotive creators, focused on the follower range where gifting actually converts rather than on big names

  • Negotiate product-for-content: they receive product, they publish agreed content. No cash fees

You supply the product from your own stock, so your cost is wholesale rather than retail.

One full production day, shot on mobile, covering several videos in a single session so you are not filming every week. Added to any month you ask for it, at 10,000 EGP for that month only.

When you need it

You do not need it every month. Most months the four scripts are filmed by your own team. Book a shooting day when there is a new product line, a seasonal push, or a batch you want produced in one go.
