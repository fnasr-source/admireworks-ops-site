Majlis Basseqat: the offline layer of your MOND system

A recurring, in-person gathering that introduces Basseqat's team to serious prospects and lets each of them decide for themselves: nothing is sold in the room, every question gets an answer with the papers on the table, and nobody from sales calls afterwards unless the guest asks. It runs on the MOND system Basseqat already uses, so invitations, check-in, the battery badge every guest wears and every follow-up live in your CRM. The first Majlis is on Saturday 10 October 2026 in Cairo. This page is the full commercial summary.

Three things worth being explicit about. The event's own costs (venue, food, sound, printing, gifts, the moderator) are paid by Basseqat directly to suppliers; our estimate for about 120 guests is 450,000 to 950,000 EGP plus a 10% contingency, and venue quotes firm it up in the first week. We measure the Majlis by contracts, not applause: feddans signed by guests within 90 days, compared with a matched group of similar prospects who were not invited. The 10 October date holds only if the four decisions under "To start" are made by Thursday 24 September.

What you get

Both options

1. The first Majlis, end to end

  • The day, designed: run of show, the battery badge (five palm stages at 20% each, set by the guest), fact cards, the "prove it to me" exchange, the risk hour, the "now I understand" wall, and the question bank for the moderator
