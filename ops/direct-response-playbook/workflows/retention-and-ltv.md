# Retention And LTV Workflow

Use this workflow once the front-end conversion path exists.

## Step-By-Step

1. Define the first success milestone after purchase.
2. Build onboarding that moves the buyer to that milestone quickly.
3. Add milestone messaging:
   - activation reminder
   - progress check-in
   - proof request or testimonial ask
   - re-engagement for inactive users
4. Add cross-sell, upsell, or continuity offers only after the first value moment.
5. Define the win-back path for churned or stalled users.
6. Review LTV by segment, not only in aggregate.

## Reusable Checklist

- [ ] Onboarding begins immediately after conversion
- [ ] First value moment is defined and measurable
- [ ] At least one reactivation sequence exists
- [ ] Referral, testimonial, or social proof capture step exists
- [ ] Upsell timing follows product usage, not arbitrary dates
- [ ] Monthly retention review is on the reporting cadence

## Store In Client Workspace

- `flows/` for activation and lifecycle logic
- `emails/` for post-purchase and win-back sequences
- `reports/` for retention and LTV review
- `sops/` for recurring retention checks
